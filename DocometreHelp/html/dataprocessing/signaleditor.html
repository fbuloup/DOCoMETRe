Signal and Category Editors

A double click on a signal of a loaded subject, in the Subjects view, opens the signal editor: it displays the trials of the signal and gives access to its cuts, markers and features. A double click on a category opens the category editor, which displays several signals for the trials of the category.

As for the data editor, the chart is drawn with OpenGL when the "SWT + OpenGL v2" real time chart library is selected in the general preferences, and with SWT otherwise. Editors show the values held by the math engine: they are updated when a processing modifies the subject.

Signal editor

The signal editor is made of:

  • the chart of the selected trials, as a function of time (or of frequency for the result of an FFT). Only the samples between the front cut and the end cut of each trial are displayed;
  • the trials list, on the right of the chart: each trial is shown with its category, for example Trial n°12 [Session1.Pointing]. Select the trials to display (several trials can be selected with Ctrl or Shift). The button between the chart and the list hides or shows the list;
  • the Informations (trials, features and markers) panel, under the chart, which can be collapsed. It is described below.

The Previous trial and Next trial buttons of the tool bar display the previous or next trial; zoom and pan buttons are also added to the tool bar.

General infos

  • signal name, subject, experiment, sample frequency (or frequency step for a frequency domain signal) and number of trials;
  • Same color for same category trials: trials of the same category are drawn with the same color;
  • Keep scale when trials change: the scales are not fitted again when other trials are selected, which makes comparisons easier;
  • Show samples: draws a point on each sample.

Trials

For the trial chosen with the Trial spinner: front cut and end cut (indexes of the first and last displayed samples), number of samples and duration (or maximum frequency).

Markers group

Markers are points of the trials (see the data model), gathered in groups identified by a label. They are usually created by functions of the library (maxima, threshold crossings...), but can also be placed by hand:

  • Group name: selects the markers group displayed and edited. The "+" button creates a new group (a dialog asks for its label, which must be a valid name), the "x" button deletes the selected group and all its markers;
  • Trial n°: lists the markers of the group, by trial and in time order. Selecting one shows its X (time) and Y (amplitude) values and the number of markers of the group in its trial, and highlights it in the chart; the "x" button deletes it.

Features

Features are single values per trial (mean, difference between two markers...). Choose a feature in Name and a trial number to read its value; the "x" button deletes the feature from all the trials.

Deleting markers or features modifies the loaded subject: save it to keep the change (see Saving subjects).

Chart commands (OpenGL)

The OpenGL chart has the same mouse and keyboard commands as the OpenGL data editor (wheel and rectangle zoom, pan, C for the cursor, R to reset the view, context menu...). In cursor mode, markers can also be edited in the chart:

  • Ctrl + left double click adds, on the point designated by the cursor, a marker of the group selected in the editor (choose or create a group first);
  • a double click on the label of a marker deletes it;
  • Ctrl + click on the label of a marker selects its group and the marker in the editor.

The context menu of the chart also shows or hides the markers and their labels.

Chart commands (SWT)

The SWT chart has the commands of the SWT data editor. When the cursor is displayed (context menu, or "Show cursor (Time charts)" in the general preferences), a double click adds a marker of the selected group on the curve of the cursor.

Category editor

The category editor displays, in a single chart, several signals of the subject for several trials of a category. On its right, it shows the criteria of the category, the list of the trials of the category and the list of the signals of the subject: select the trials and the signals to display. "Same colors for same trial" draws all the signals of a trial with the same color. Markers of the displayed signals are shown, but cannot be edited in this editor.
