Loading Subjects

In the Analyse perspective, the data of a subject can only be displayed and processed once the subject has been loaded in the math engine. Loading reads all the data files of the subject and gathers them in the data model: one matrix of values per signal, with one line per trial.

Selecting the experiment

The Subjects view shows one experiment at a time. Use the Select experiment button of the tool bar (or of the context menu) and choose the experiment in the list. Its name is displayed in the status bar and is remembered for the next start of DOCoMETRe.

Loading and unloading subjects

Start the math engine, select one or several subjects in the Subjects view (or in the Experiments view) and press Load/unload selected subjects. Selected subjects that are not loaded are loaded, the others are unloaded. Once loaded, a subject is decorated with an overlay and can be expanded to show its signals, categories and events.

The following data files of the subject are read (see Supported data files formats):

  • the samples files of all its trials (data acquired with DOCoMETRe);
  • ADW files, Optitrack files and files of data organized in columns (data imported in the subject). Column data files can only be loaded with Matlab.

Trials numbering and categories

For samples files, the trials of all the sessions of the subject are numbered one after the other: the sessions are taken in alphabetical order of their names, and the trials of a session follow those of the previous session. For example, with a session A_Familiarization of 10 trials and a session B_Test of 30 trials, trial 1 of session B_Test is trial 11 of the subject.

The trials are grouped in categories: a category is created for each couple (session, process), named SessionName.ProcessName (preceded by the data files prefix of the session if any). A category thus gathers all the trials of a session run with the same process, that is with the same experimental condition. Categories are listed under the loaded subject and are used to select trials in the editors.

Signals of a subject have as many lines as the total number of trials of its sessions, and as many columns as the longest trial: shorter trials are completed with zeros, or with their last value when "Padd with last value rather than zero" is checked in the Math Engine preferences. The real number of samples of each trial is kept in the NbSamples element of the signal.

Loading from saved data

When a subject has been saved, DOCoMETRe asks whether it must be loaded from its raw data files (answer Yes: the results of previous processing are not loaded) or from its saved data (answer No: the subject is restored as it was saved, with its processed signals, markers and features). Check "Always load subject from saved data" in the Math Engine preferences to load saved data without question.

Saving subjects

Processing modifies the data of the loaded subjects in the math engine only. A modified subject is decorated with a "modified" overlay. To keep these modifications, select the subject in the Subjects view and use File > Save (or Save all). The data are written in the folder of the subject:

  • with Python: save.data and data_N.numpy files;
  • with Matlab: save.mat.

When modified subjects are unloaded (or when the math engine is stopped), a dialog lists them: check the subjects to save, then press Terminate (Cancel cancels the unloading).

Saved data only depend on the math engine that wrote them: a subject saved with Python must be loaded with Python to retrieve its saved data.

Merging data of subjects

Merge data (context menu of the Subjects view) copies the signals of a loaded subject into another loaded subject, for example to gather data recorded by two different systems for the same participant. In the dialog, add couples "Merge data from" / "To" subjects. A merge is refused when the two subjects do not have the same number of trials, when a signal of the first subject already exists in the second one, or when a subject is merged with itself; the reason is written in the Console view.
