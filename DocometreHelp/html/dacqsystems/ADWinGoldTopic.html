ADwin Gold and Gold II

ADwin Gold and ADwin Gold II are compact systems: the processor and the inputs/outputs (analog inputs, analog outputs, digital inputs/outputs) are in a single box. Read ADwin DACQ Systems first for the installation of the ADwin software and the connection of the system.

Declaring the inputs and outputs

Although the inputs and outputs of a Gold system are on the main board, they are declared in DOCoMETRe as modules, like on a Pro system: add one Analog inputs, one Analog outputs and one Digital inputs/outputs module according to your needs, then add their channels. As there is only one module of each type, keep the module number to 1.

  • Analog inputs: the channel number is the number of the input of the system. The amplitude range of the module and the gain of each channel must match the settings of your system.
  • Digital inputs/outputs: the 32 channels are configured by groups of 8 (1-8, 9-16, 17-24 and 25-32). When one channel of a group is declared as an output (Inp./Out. column), the whole group is configured as outputs: do not mix inputs and outputs in the same group. A usual choice is channels 1 to 16 as inputs and 17 to 32 as outputs.
  • CAN and RS232 interfaces are available when the option is installed on your system.

Check the hardware documentation of your system (number of analog inputs, ranges, options) and the wiring with the calibration and monitoring dialog.

Gold or Gold II ?

The Gold II processor is much faster: it allows higher global frequencies and more computations in each period of the real time loop. The processes are the same for both variants: to move an experiment from a Gold to a Gold II, change the CPU type and the boot loader file of the DACQ configuration, then compile the processes again.
