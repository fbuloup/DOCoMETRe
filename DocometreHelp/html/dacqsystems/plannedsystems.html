Planned Systems

The systems and modules below are being considered for future versions of DOCoMETRe. They are not supported yet: they can't be selected in a DACQ configuration.

  • MCP4725: 12 bits digital to analog converter on the I2C bus, to give true analog outputs to the Arduino Uno;
  • Arduino Portenta Machine Control: industrial controller with 24V analog and digital inputs/outputs;
  • Teensy 4.0: fast micro-controller board (600 MHz ARM Cortex-M7).

The currently supported systems are listed in Supported DACQ Systems.
